Engineering philosophy

Built tooutlast the brief.

Every BAC aircraft is engineered not just to meet today's operational demands, but to adapt to the routes, economics and environmental standards of the next four decades. We design for the long runway.

Composite-first construction
Over 72% of structural airframe weight in advanced carbon-fibre composites, reducing empty weight and improving fatigue resistance.
Next-generation propulsion
Ultra-high bypass ratio turbofans on every variant, with SAF-100 compatibility and hydrogen-hybrid readiness built into the architecture.
British manufacturing
Final assembly at Bristol Filton. Wings from Broughton. Engines from Derby. Every major component from British soil.
