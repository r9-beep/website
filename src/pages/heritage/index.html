The Bristol inheritance · 1910–1959
1910

Filton takes flight

Sir George White founds the British and Colonial Aeroplane Company in a converted tram shed at Filton, Bristol — the site where BAC aircraft are assembled today.

Filton
1949

The jet age begins

In July the de Havilland Comet makes the world's first jet airliner flight from Hatfield. Weeks later, the giant Bristol Brabazon lifts off from Filton's new runway.

World first
1952

First jet passenger service

The Comet enters service with BOAC, flying the world's first scheduled jet airliner service, London to Johannesburg.

World first
The original BAC · 1960–1977
1960

British Aircraft Corporation

Bristol, English Electric and Vickers-Armstrongs combine their aircraft interests to form the British Aircraft Corporation, joined soon after by Hunting.

Consolidation
1963

The One-Eleven flies

The BAC One-Eleven — rear-engined, T-tailed and built for short-haul — makes its first flight. It goes on to serve airlines on six continents.

The original 1-11
1969

Concorde 002 at Filton

The British-assembled Concorde prototype makes its maiden flight from Filton, a month after its French sister ship took to the air in Toulouse.

Supersonic
1976

Concorde enters service

Supersonic passenger service begins — the pinnacle of British and French aerospace collaboration.

Supersonic
1977

Into British Aerospace

The original British Aircraft Corporation is nationalised and merged into British Aerospace. The BAC name falls silent.

End of an era
The long pause · 1977–2023
2003

Concorde comes home

The final Concorde flight lands at Filton. For two decades, Britain builds wings, engines and systems for the world's airliners — but not the airliners themselves.

Filton
The revival · 2024 onwards
2024

A new British Aircraft Corporation

BAC is incorporated in Bristol with a single ambition: to design, build and certify a complete family of British airliners.

BAC programme
2025

2-22 launched at Paris

The 757 replacement the industry has waited two decades for is launched at the Paris Air Show.

BAC programme
2026

First metal cut

Production of the first 1-11NXT flight-test airframe begins at Filton.

1-11NXT enters service

The spiritual successor to the One-Eleven enters service with its launch customer.

London to Sydney, nonstop

The 4-44ULR's first flight: Filton to Sydney in a single leg.
