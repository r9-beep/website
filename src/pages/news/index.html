15 Sep 2026 · Programme

First metal cut for the 1-11NXT at Filton

+

Production of the first 1-11NXT flight-test airframe began this week at Filton, Bristol, as the first titanium wing-attachment fittings were machined and the first composite fuselage barrel arrived from Belfast.

Sixty-odd years ago the original One-Eleven took shape a few miles from here. Today we start the aircraft that succeeds it.

The airframe, MSN 001, will join the 1-11NXT flight-test fleet. Final assembly of the first aircraft is scheduled to begin in the first half of 2027.

21 Jul 2026 · Programme

The 4-44ULR cabin, revealed at Farnborough

+

A full-scale cabin mock-up of the 4-44ULR debuted at the Farnborough International Airshow, showcasing the dedicated wellness zone, light-therapy bar and the circadian lighting system designed for 20-hour sectors.

The mock-up features the 1-2-1 business cabin, a 2-3-2 premium economy and the 3-3-3 economy cabin with 33-inch pitch — the most generous standard economy pitch of any long-haul aircraft on offer.

6 May 2026 · Programme

Wideboy wing passes ultimate load at Broughton

+

The 3-33 Wideboy's static-test composite wing box was taken to 150% of limit load at Broughton without failure, clearing a key milestone ahead of schedule.

Data from the test will be used to finalise the wing design for the 3-33 and to validate the shared wing technology used on the 4-44 family.

12 Mar 2026 · Customers

Launch customer signs for up to 40 aircraft

+

A European flag carrier, to be named at a later date, has signed a memorandum of understanding for up to 40 BAC aircraft: firm orders for 1-11NXT and 2-22 aircraft, with options covering the 3-33 Wideboy.

Airlines have told us for years that the middle of the market is under-served. This is them telling us with signatures.
18 Nov 2025 · Corporate

An all-British propulsion supply chain

+

BAC confirmed that every engine across the family will be built in Derby, completing a supply chain in which every major airframe and propulsion component is sourced from the United Kingdom.

16 Jun 2025 · Programme

2-22 launched at the Paris Air Show

+

BAC formally launched the 2-22, a 202-seat, 7,200 km medium narrow-body designed as the definitive replacement for the Boeing 757 — and the first clean-sheet British airliner launched in a generation.

1 Oct 2024 · Corporate

British Aircraft Corporation incorporated in Bristol

+

A new British Aircraft Corporation was incorporated in Bristol, with a founding mission to design, build and certify a complete family of British airliners — from a 162-seat narrow-body to a 19,000 km ultra-long-range flagship.

Britain invented the jet airliner. It is time we reminded the world of that.
