FAQ

Good questions.

When is the earliest delivery slot?

The 1-11NXT enters service in 2028, followed by the 2-22 in 2029, the 3-33 and 4-44 in 2030 and the 4-44ULR in 2032. Early-production slots are allocated in order of firm commitment.

Do you offer leasing?

Yes. We work with established lessors and offer direct operating leases on early-production aircraft, including flexible return conditions for launch operators.

What does support look like?

Every delivery includes an entry-into-service team, a 24/7 operations centre in Bristol and regional support hubs in Singapore and Dubai, with power-by-the-hour options on airframe and engines.

Can we visit Filton?

Customer and press visits to the final assembly line can be arranged with two weeks' notice. Use the form above and choose the closest enquiry type.

Are you the original BAC?

No. The original British Aircraft Corporation (1960–1977) became part of British Aerospace. We are a new company, founded in 2024, carrying the name with pride and no affiliation.
