Commonality

Designed as a family.

Fewer type ratings, shared spares and one training pipeline. Commonality is how a new manufacturer competes on total cost of ownership from day one.

01 · Narrow-body

1-11NXT & 2-22

A common flight deck, shared systems and a single maintenance training course. Airlines can fly a 162-seat Bristol–Faro and a 202-seat Heathrow–Boston with the same crews.

02 · Widebody

3-33 & 4-44

One type rating across two widebodies. The 3-33 Wideboy and the 4-44 share fly-by-wire laws, cockpit layout and 70% of their line-replaceable units.

03 · Ultra-long-range

4-44 & 4-44ULR

The ULR is built on the 4-44 airframe with a revised fuel system and four engines — pilots convert with a short differences course.
